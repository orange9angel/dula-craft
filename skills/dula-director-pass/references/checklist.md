# Director checklist (score every full draft; note time stamps)

1. Opening: frame 0 presentable (thumbnail); the first 10 s give a reason to keep watching.
2. Monotony: no single set/framing dominates >40% of the tile; a new image at least every section.
3. Rhythm: motion energy follows the song (quiet/loud); cuts land on beats; one rhythmic surprise (freeze, drop, hold).
4. Motion quality: nothing glides, jitters or jumps unless it is a deliberate cut; no walk cycles on the wrong axis.
5. Physics/scale: placement plausible; nearer = bigger; props rest on what holds them; nothing blocks a used path.
6. Hierarchy: the lead is findable but not pasted-on; solid vs ghost used on purpose; colour reserved for the story.
7. Story beat: the key event (cause then effect) reads on one viewing; the ending lands on the last lyric.
8. Close-ups: alive (hair, light, passing world), not still pictures; faces consistent across ages/shots.
9. Earlier rejections recorded in the film's notes are not violated again after local repairs.
10. One memorable image per section; cut a device that does not serve the song.
11. Ground and sources: every figure's feet stand in the plate's walkable band; every painted source of motion (steam, flame,
    water, leaves) actually moves.
12. Lyrics and titles: font matches the film's look (checked in the ffmpeg fontselect log), readable on the brightest shot.
